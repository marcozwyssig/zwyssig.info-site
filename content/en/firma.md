## About us

Zwyssig Informatik GmbH was founded in 2005. Since its founding, it has built an excellent reputation through a consistent focus on customer needs, combined with ongoing knowledge building and transfer. We see our role as advisors and developers in a holistic context - we offer integrative solutions that create sustainable added value on both the business and the technological level. The correct identification of process needs and intensive project support, from modeling through to quality assurance, are of great importance in this context.

We are delighted about the growing number of satisfied customers and hope to continue securing lasting business success through excellent software solutions in the future.
